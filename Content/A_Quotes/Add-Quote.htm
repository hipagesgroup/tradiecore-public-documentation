Creating a quote manually

A quote is the price you give a customer for a job, and should include the cost of labour and materials. You can send several quotes for the same job. A quote can also include a deposit amount for your customer to pay before work begins.

Quotes move through three statuses: Draft, Sent, and Approved. These update automatically and can't be changed manually. A quote's status is also tied to its job's status:

  • When a quote is sent, the quote status changes to Sent and the job status changes to Quoted, regardless of how many quotes have been sent for that job.

  • When a quote is approved, either by the customer selecting Approve or by paying a deposit, the quote status changes to Approved and the job status changes to Hired.

You can add line items, apply tax settings, and include attachments if needed. You can also create a quote using Smart Quotes, hipages' AI-assisted quote creation feature.

Note:

You don’t need to send a separate invoice or receipt when a deposit is paid. When you send the final invoice after the job is complete, the deposit amount is automatically deducted from the total. Your customer only pays the remaining balance shown on the invoice

A quote can only be edited before it has been sent to the customer. Once sent, it cannot be changed. If you need to make changes after sending, you'll need to create a new quote or a copy of the existing one. See Manual quote actions for full details.

Creating a quote

On the app

  1. Select Jobs from the navigation bar, and open a job.

  2. In the Quotes section, select Create Quote.

  3. Then to create a manual quote, choose Skip to quote manually.

    The quote creation screen is displayed, with the customer's name and job details pre-filled based on the job.

On the web

  1. Select Jobs , and open a job.

  2. In the Quotes section, select add .

    The quote creation screen is displayed, with the customer's details and your tax preferences pre-filled.

Managing your quote details

When you create a new quote, the customer's details, quote description, and tax setting are shown together at the top of the quote.

To update or change customer details:

Only available on hipages for Business app. On the web, a quote's customer details are taken directly from the job and can't be edited from the quote.

  1. Select the customer name at the top of the quote.

  2. Select Edit.

  3. From here, you can either:

    • Update the existing customer’s name, phone number, email, or address or,

    • Select Search Existing Customers to find and select a different customer from your list

To update the quote description:

While not required, adding a description is good practice. It helps build trust with your customer and shows you've thought about the job.

  1. Select the Quote Description field.

  2. Enter a clear, concise description of the job.

To update tax preferences:

  1. In the app, select the Tax field.

    A pop-up will appear with tax options:

    • Tax Inclusive (default): Amounts entered already include GST. The GST will be calculated and shown separately but not added again.

    • Tax Exclusive: GST is calculated and added on top of the amounts you enter. [See: How GST is applied to your quotes and invoices]

  2. Choose the setting that best matches how you usually quote.

  3. On the web select the Price is drop down, then choose the tax option you want to apply.

Adding items to your quote

  1. In the Items section, select add .

  2. In the Name field, type the name or code of the item.

    • If you’ve used the item before, select it from the list of saved items to pre-fill its details.

    • To create a new item, select Create a new line item. [See: Creating a new item for more details.]

  3. Adding an item Description is optional, but useful if your item names are codes or abbreviations, since the description gives your customer more context

  4. Enter the Quantity and Unit Price. If needed, adjust the Tax Rate (default is 10% GST; select None if no GST applies).

  5. Select Add to include the item in the quote.

    If you select Cancel, the item won’t be saved.

As you add items, the Subtotal, GST, and Total for the quote are calculated automatically.

Note:

Changes made to an item here override the saved version of that item once the quote is saved. If you need different versions of the same item, create new items with unique names.

Adding a deposit (optional)

If you want your customer to commit to the job before work begins, you can request a deposit.

  1. Go to the Deposits section.

  2. Choose how to set the deposit:

    • Percentage of total - Enter the percentage of the total you’d like the customer to pay.

    • Deposit amount - Enter a specific dollar value.

    hipages for business will automatically calculate the corresponding value in the other field. For example: if you enter 25% as the percentage, the deposit amount will be calculated and displayed automatically.

Note:

If you convert a quote that includes a deposit into an invoice, the deposit amount is automatically deducted. If you create a new invoice instead, you can manually add and deduct the deposit amount. [See Creating a new invoice for more information]

Previewing a quote

The Preview option on the Quotes screen, lets you view the PDF version of the quote exactly as your customer will see it.

To preview a quote: 

  1. Select Preview at the top of the Quote screen, on the web select the preview icon .

  2. On the app, you can send it directly to your customer by selecting Send via email.

    On the web, close the preview to go back to the quote screen.

Sending or saving a quote

Select Send to email the quote to your customer. [see: Emailing an invoice or quote to a customer]

When sent, your customer receives an email with a PDF copy of the quote, including any deposit amount required.

On the app

You can either:

  • Go back to the job, by selecting Associated Job.

  • Select Add to schedule to go to the scheduling screen, where you can set up an appointment for the job. [See: Adding an appointment to a job].

Once your customer approves the quote, you'll also receive a confirmation email and a phone notification.

On the web

After sending, a confirmation screen displays with Go back to joboption.
