Managing your package and credit usage

The Usage screen is where you can keep track of the following:

  • Your credit usage and balance for the month

  • Buy a credit pack (lead pack) if you need more credits

  • Upgrade your package

  • Information about your current package

  • The renewal date of your package

Note:

How long your package credits are valid depends on your package. For packages of $449 + GST per month or more, credits are valid for 3 months. For packages under $449 + GST per month, credits are valid for 2 months. You can use your current month’s credits and, if needed, credits from your next monthly allocation. Unused credits roll over until they expire. Credit packs are valid until the end of your current subscription term, or until you’ve used the credits, whichever comes first.

Viewing your lead credit balance

This section shows your current credit balance, your current package cost, and the package renewal date.

To view and track your hipages credit:

  • Select Profile → My Package → Credit Balance.

    The Usage screen is then displayed.

Credit packs are only available in set sizes. You can’t buy a custom number of credits, either in the app or through our Support team. Choose the pack that’s closest to the number of credits you need. The available packs are shown on the Upgrade screen.

Upgrading your package

  1. On the Usage screen, select Upgrade package.

    The Upgrade screen is displayed.

  2. Under Select a package to upgrade, choose the package you want. Packages have a grey background and show the monthly cost, monthly credits, average number of leads and number of postcodes.

  3. Select Continue to payment.

  4. On the payments screen, choose a saved card or add a new card.

  5. Review the terms and conditions, then select the confirmation checkbox.

  6. Select Complete purchase.

Buying a credit pack (lead pack)

  1. On the Usage screen, select + Add credits.

    The Upgrade screen is displayed.

  2. Scroll down past the packages to Alternatively, you can top up for further usage and select the credit pack you want. Credit packs have a white background.

  3. Select Continue to payment.

  4. On the payments screen, choose a saved card or add a new card.

  5. When you’re asked whether you want to upgrade your package, select No thanks, continue to buy only the credit pack.

  6. Review the terms and conditions, then select the confirmation checkbox.

  7. Select Complete purchase.

    The credits are added to your balance.

Checking your credit breakdown

The Credits Breakdown section of the Usage screen shows you a detailed breakdown of:

  • Your monthly credits

  • If you have added a credit pack

  • Additional loyalty bonus credits

  • Expiry dates

Checking your usage history

The Usage History section shows a daily breakdown of how your lead credits are used. You can see how many credits were spent on each accepted lead, along with your remaining lead credit balance, helping you track usage and manage your spend more effectively.
