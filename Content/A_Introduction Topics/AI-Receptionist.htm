Getting started with the AI Receptionist

Early access

Never miss a job because you couldn't get to the phone. The AI Receptionist answers calls when you can't, takes down the job details, and turns each call into an enquiry you can action in the app.

Before you start:

The AI Receptionist is a paid add-on for account owners on a current hipages for business package. Your first month is free, then it's $79 per month (plus GST). The free month is available once per account.

How it works

When you sign up, you get your own dedicated virtual number. The AI Receptionist answers every call to that number. You can use it in two ways:

  • As your business number. Add it to your website, ads, vehicle or business cards so new customers call it directly.

  • As a backup for your mobile. Set up call forwarding so calls you miss on your own phone go to the AI Receptionist after a set number of seconds.

You can use one or both. Call forwarding is optional.

What happens on a call

The AI Receptionist greets the caller, lets them know it's an AI receptionist, then asks about the job. It captures:

  • the caller's name and phone number

  • their suburb

  • the type of job and what they need

  • how urgent it is

  • when they're available

If the caller wants someone to come out and quote, the AI Receptionist can suggest a time for a quote inspection. It checks the caller's availability against your Schedule in the hipages for business app.

Keep your Schedule up to date

The AI Receptionist can only see appointments in your hipages for business Schedule. It can't see any other calendars, such as your phone's calendar or Google Calendar. To avoid double bookings, add all your appointments to your Schedule in the app.

The booking is tentative. Nothing is locked in until you confirm it or choose a different time.

If the call isn't about a job, for example a supplier or someone asking about an apprenticeship, the AI Receptionist takes a message instead.

What you get

After the call, the AI Receptionist creates an enquiry with a summary of the call and the full transcript. You'll get a push notification straight away. Tap it to open the enquiry in the app. We also send you an email as a backup.

Urgent enquiries are labelled Urgent so you can spot them quickly.

From the enquiry, you can call the customer back, confirm or change a tentative booking, or convert the enquiry into a job.

Setting up your AI Receptionist

The first time you open the AI Receptionist, you'll set up your receptionist and then activate your free month. It only takes a few minutes.

Already set up?

You can change your receptionist's voice, your business details, what your receptionist asks callers, its greetings and goodbyes, and your booking guidelines at any time. For more information, see Changing your AI Receptionist settings.

Personalise your receptionist

To personalise your receptionist:

  1. From the To Do screen, tap the inbox icon in the top right corner.

  2. Swipe through the screens to see how the AI Receptionist works, then select Get early access.

  3. Choose your receptionist.

    Select Female or Male, then choose a voice. Each voice has its own personality, for example Emily (calm and natural).

    Tap the play icon to hear how each one sounds.

  4. Select Next: Confirm your details.

  5. Check your preferred name and preferred business name.

    Your receptionist uses these to introduce you and your business during calls. Update them if needed.

Activate your free month

To activate your free month:

  1. Review the offer, then select Activate AI Receptionist.

  2. Select your payment method, or select Use a new card to add one.

  3. Read the terms and conditions, then tick the box to agree to them.

  4. Select Complete purchase. There's nothing to pay today.

We'll process your request and create your dedicated number. When you see You're good to go, your number is ready to use. Select Copy to copy it.

From here, select Ok, got it to go to your enquiries, or Configure settings to open your AI Receptionist settings.

Your number also appears on the Enquiries screen under How to start using your AI Receptionist. From there, you can copy your number or select Forward your missed calls to set up call forwarding.

If you stop partway through

Your progress is saved. Next time you open your enquiries, the Continue set up screen shows which steps you've completed. Select the button at the bottom of the screen to pick up where you left off.
