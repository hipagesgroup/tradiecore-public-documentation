Completing ID checks in hipages for business

You may need to complete two separate identity checks on hipages for business: a hipages ID check, if your business works in a category that requires one, and a Stripe ID check when you set up payments.

hipages ID check

The hipages ID check confirms that a real person is behind your account before you can accept leads. It helps protect your business from anyone using your publicly available ABN or licence details to impersonate you. The check is free, takes a few minutes, and only confirms your identity. It isn't a police or trade licence check.

The check is handled by GBG, our secure third-party identity provider, using official Australian government records. hipages receives the result of the check, never copies of your documents.

We'll email and SMS you when your business needs an ID check, for example when you sign up or add a category that requires one. This applies to both trial and paid accounts.

Who completes the ID check

  • Sole traders and family partnerships: The account holder verifies their own identity.

  • Australian private companies (Pty Ltd): The billing contact chooses one current company director, and that director verifies their identity. The list of directors comes from the ASIC register.

What you'll need

You can verify your identity with either:

  • Two Australian ID documents, current and in your name: driver licence, Medicare card, Australian passport, or a foreign passport with a valid Australian visa.

  • ConnectID, which verifies you through your bank's app. We never receive your banking details.

Only have one ID document? Contact support, and our team can review alternatives such as a utility bill or rates notice.

Note:

Use your legal name exactly as it appears on your ID.

Completing the ID check as a sole trader or family partnership

  1. Go to the ID Verification page. To get there, open the link in our email or SMS, select the banner in your account, or select Profile → My Business → ID Verification.

  2. Select Complete ID Check.

  3. Verify with two ID documents or ConnectID on GBG's secure page.

Once you're verified, your account unlocks automatically and you can start accepting leads.

Completing the ID check for an Australian private company

As the billing contact:

  1. Go to the ID Verification page. To get there, open the link in our email or SMS, or select Profile → My Business → ID Verification.

  2. Choose a director from the list of current directors registered with ASIC, and enter their email address and mobile number.

  3. Complete Director ID Check in the app and Start Director ID Check in the web.

    App Web

The director then receives a secure link to complete the check. Once they're verified, the account unlocks for everyone on it.

If you chose the wrong director, select Change next to their name before they finish verifying. You can do this once. If you're both the billing contact and a director, select Complete ID Check to verify straight away.

Until your ID check is approved

You can log in and use your account, but you can't accept leads. We'll send email and SMS reminders until the check is done. If your check fails, you'll be taken back to the ID Verification page, where you can select Contact Support for help.

Once you're verified, you won't be asked again unless you reactivate your account after a long break, or your company's details change.

Need help?

  • Link didn't arrive or isn't working? Check your junk folder, then select Resend link on the ID Verification page.

  • Anything else, such as having only one ID document, being locked out, a failed check, or a director who has left the company? Select Contact Support on the ID Verification page.

To call or email us instead, see Calling hipages for business support and Emailing hipages for business support.

Stripe ID check

To receive payments through hipages Payments, you'll need to set up a Stripe account. As part of this, Stripe asks for information to complete a Document Verification Service (DVS) check, sometimes referred to as a 100-point ID check, before your Stripe account is fully active.

Note:

This is a legal requirement under the Australian government's Know Your Customer process, not a hipages for business policy.
