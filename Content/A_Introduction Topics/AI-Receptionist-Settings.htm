Changing your AI Receptionist settings

After you've set up your AI Receptionist, you can change how it works at any time. You can update your business details, choose a different voice, decide what details it captures from customers, customise how it answers and ends calls, and set when it can book appointments.

Opening your settings

To open your AI Receptionist settings:

  1. From the To Do screen, open your enquiries, then tap the menu icon in the top right corner.

  2. Select AI Receptionist settings.

The AI Receptionist screen shows your virtual number at the top. To copy it, select Copy. To go back to your enquiries, select View inbox.

After you make changes on any settings screen, select Done.

Setting up call forwarding

Select Set up call forwarding to choose how you'd like to use your virtual number. There are two options, and they work differently:

Option

Which calls the AI Receptionist answers

What you need to do
Forward calls Only calls you miss on your existing mobile number Set up call forwarding with your phone provider
Use as direct line Every call to your virtual number Share your virtual number with customers

Because every call to your virtual number goes to the AI Receptionist, keep giving out your own mobile number to anyone you want to speak to directly.

To forward missed calls

Select Forward calls. You can set up forwarding by dialling a forwarding code, or through your phone settings. For more information, see Setting up call forwarding.

If you're on a prepaid plan or not sure whether forwarding works with your provider, select See the detailed guides.

To use your number as a direct line

Select Use as direct line. No forwarding is needed. Add your virtual number to your website, ads, vehicle or business cards, and the AI Receptionist will answer every call to it.

Updating your business details

Select Your business details to check the details your receptionist uses to introduce you and your business.

To update your business details

You can change:

  • Your preferred name: how your receptionist refers to you

  • Preferred Business Name: how your receptionist refers to your business

The screen also shows the areas you work in and your service categories. These come from your lead settings and can't be changed here. To change them, see:

  • Managing your coverage area

  • Managing your service categories

Changing your receptionist's voice

Select Voice & personality to choose a different voice for your receptionist. Each voice has its own name and personality, for example Emily (warm and friendly).

To change your receptionist's voice

  1. Select Female or Male.

  2. Tap the play icon next to a voice to hear how it sounds.

  3. Select the voice you want to use.

  4. Select Done.

Choosing what details are captured

Select Enquiry capture to see the details your receptionist asks for on every job enquiry. By default, it asks for:

  • the customer's contact name, number and email

  • the job location and address

  • a detailed description of the job

  • how time-sensitive and urgent the job is

You can add your own details to the list, for example the customer's budget.

To add a detail

  1. On the Enquiry details screen, select Add to script, or tap the plus icon in the top right corner.

  2. In Detail, enter a short name for the detail, for example Budget.

  3. In What should your receptionist ask?, describe the question. For example, "Ask if the customer has a budget in mind."

    You can type it, or tap the microphone icon to say it.

  4. Tap the tick to save it.

The detail appears under Added.

To edit or remove a detail

Tap the menu icon next to the detail, then select Edit or Remove.

Customising greetings and goodbyes

Select Greetings & goodbyes to change what your receptionist says when it answers and ends a call.

  • How calls should be answered: the greeting callers hear first

  • How calls should be ended: what your receptionist says before hanging up

To change a greeting or goodbye

  1. Tap the pencil icon on the message you want to change.

    The Edit Greeting panel opens.

  2. Edit the message.

    To include your receptionist's name, type {voice_name}. To include your business name, type {business_name}.

  3. Tap the tick to save it.

Setting your booking guidelines

Select Booking guidelines to choose how your receptionist handles appointment requests from customers, and when it can book them in.

Choosing a booking preference

Under Booking preference, select one of the following:

  • Capture preference only: your receptionist asks the customer when they're free and sends you the request. You then schedule the appointment yourself.

  • Book in tentatively: your receptionist reserves a time in your Schedule and tells the customer you'll follow up to confirm. You can then confirm the booking or arrange a different time. For more information, see Managing your AI Receptionist enquiries.

Book in tentatively is selected by default.

Setting when your receptionist can book

Under When your receptionist can book, set the days and hours you're available for appointments. Your receptionist only suggests times within these hours, and checks your Schedule before booking. Times are based on the timezone shown under Timezone.

To add your available hours

  1. Under Selected availability, select Add.

    The Preferred hours panel opens.

  2. Select the first day, and if needed, the last day of the range. For example, Monday to Thursday.

  3. Enter the start and end times, and select am or pm for each.

  4. To add a different set of days or hours, select Add another preference, then repeat steps 2 and 3.

  5. Select Done.

To remove your available hours

Tap the X next to the days and hours you want to remove.
